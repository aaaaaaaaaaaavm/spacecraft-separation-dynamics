# Provenance and limits

The two-body impulse, recoil, and internal-mass screens are extracted and generalised from
[`orbital-deployment-trade-study`](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/orbital-deployment-trade-study)
and the release analyses in [VOLLEY](https://github.com/aaaaaaaaaaaavm/VOLLEY).

No atmosphere, J2, covariance, contact dynamics, flexible modes, or controller response is
included. The outputs are preliminary trade screens, not flight or conjunction products.
